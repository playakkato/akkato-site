Help and resources

For people in the United States. Details can change, so check each organization's website for the latest.

Akkato is just a game, but the people who play it are more important. If you or someone you know needs support, these free services are available.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.

Thinking about suicide or self-harm, or in emotional crisis

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or chat online. Available 24/7. The Lifeline also lists support for veterans, Spanish speakers, and American Indian and Alaska Native communities.
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741, any time.

Domestic violence

  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: call 800-799-7233, text START to 88788, or chat online. Available 24/7. If you are worried someone may see your activity, consider using a private browsing window or a device only you can access.

Hunger and food help

  • USDA National Hunger Hotline: call 1-866-348-6479 (1-866-3-HUNGRY), Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern, or text 914-342-7744. They connect people with food banks, meal sites and assistance programs.
  • 211: dial 211 or visit the site to find local food programs and other community help.
