Pop. Fall. Relax.

Akkato is a calm tile-matching puzzle. Tap groups of matching blocks, watch the rest settle into place, and unwind. No ads, no account, and it works fully offline.

Play in your browser

On iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android: open the menu and choose Install app.

No ads, ever

Nothing interrupts your game. Your progress saves on your device.

Plays offline

On a plane, on the bus, anywhere. No connection needed.

Made for every eye

Color-blind friendly and high-contrast palettes, a distinct shape on every block, light and dark themes.

Three ways to play

Levels give you a goal and a move limit, with up to three stars. Endless has no pressure at all. Timed is a 60-second sprint for when you want a little more challenge.

Easy to learn, satisfying to master

Pop seven or more blocks to make a bomb, chain bombs for bigger blasts, and fill your combo meter to earn Swap, Dissolve and Paint power-ups when a board gets tricky.

About Akkato

Akkato is made by one independent developer, with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic) for code, writing and design drafts. Every design decision and every release is reviewed and approved by a person.

Akkato is free of ads and works offline.
